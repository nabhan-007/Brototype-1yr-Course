PHRASES TO LEARN
Here are some phrases that can help make your public speaking more engaging:
● "Imagine if..." - Encourages the audience to visualise a scenario.
● "Picture this..." - Similar to "imagine if," helps create a mental image.
● "Let me share a story..." - Introduces a personal anecdote or narrative.
● "Have you ever wondered..." - Sparks curiosity and invites reflection.
● "Think back to a time when..." - Encourages the audience to recall a relevant
experience.
● "Now, here's the interesting part..." - Builds anticipation for a key point or
revelation.
● "But here's the twist..." - Adds a surprising or unexpected element to your
narrative.
● "Let's break it down..." - Prepares the audience for a detailed explanation.
● "So, what does this mean for us?" - Encourages reflection on the relevance of
your topic.
● "In conclusion..." - Signals the end of your presentation and summarises key
points. Show less
