Weekly Project Time Planning & Execution Strategy
Create a clear and realistic weekly plan for your project that outlines how you will manage your time and progress effectively. The plan should focus on dividing your project into meaningful chunks of work for the week, ensuring consistent progress toward completion.

You should:

- List the major areas or features you will work on during the week
- Break each area into actionable tasks
- Define what “completion” looks like for each task
- Prioritize tasks based on importance and dependency
- Identify potential blockers and how you plan to approach them

The goal is to demonstrate your ability to plan and manage your own workflow efficiently. By the end of the week, you should be able to show how your planned tasks were executed and how closely you followed your schedule.
