GRAMMAR LESSON FOR THIS WEEK 11. Capitalisation
Capitalization is when we use big letters at the beginning of sentences, for names of
people, places, or things, and for the pronoun "I." It helps us know when a new sentence
starts and when we're talking about something specific
For example, in the sentence "My name is Sara," we use a capital letter for "My" because
it's the start of the sentence. We also use a capital letter for "Sara" because it's a name.
So, capitalization is like giving important words a special hat to show that they're special! Show less

PHRASES TO LEARN
Here are some important phrases that can be helpful for online meetings:
Starting the Meeting:
● "Good [morning/afternoon/evening], everyone. Let's get started."
● "Hello, and welcome to today's meeting."
● "Thank you all for joining us today."
Introducing Yourself:
● "For those who may not know me, my name is [Your Name]."
● "I'm [Your Name], and I'll be leading today's discussion."
● "Hi, I'm [Your Name], and I'll be facilitating the meeting."
Addressing Technical Issues:
● "If anyone is experiencing technical difficulties, please let us know in the
chat."
● "If you're having trouble hearing or seeing me, try refreshing your browser
or reconnecting to the call."
Managing Interruptions:
● "Excuse me, I believe someone was trying to speak. Please go ahead."
● "Let's ensure everyone has a chance to speak. Please raise your hand or use
the 'raise hand' feature if you'd like to contribute."
Encouraging Participation:
● "We'd love to hear from everyone. Please feel free to unmute yourself and
share your thoughts."
● "Does anyone have any questions or comments on [topic]?"
● "We value everyone's input, so please don't hesitate to speak up."
Clarifying Points:
● "Could you please clarify what you meant by [statement]?"
● "I want to make sure I understand correctly. Are you saying [rephrase]?"
Wrapping Up:
● "We have a few minutes left. Are there any final thoughts or questions
before we conclude?"
● "Thank you all for your valuable contributions. Let's summarise our key
takeaways before we adjourn."
Closing the Meeting:
● "That concludes today's meeting. Thank you all for your time and
participation."
● "Before we go, does anyone have any announcements or reminders?"
● "Our next meeting is scheduled for [date]. Please mark your calendars
accordingly."
Remember to adapt these phrases to suit the tone and purpose of your specific meeting.
Clear communication and effective collaboration are key to successful online meetings.
Practise using these terms during your reviews. Show less
